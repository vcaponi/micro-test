Microeconomics — Externalities
Negative Production Externality
Identify and click on the socially efficient equilibrium in the graph below.

Background

Some production activities generate side-effects that affect parties outside the transaction. These spillover costs are captured by the marginal external damage (MED), which shifts the social marginal cost above the private one. The graph displays demand, private marginal cost (PMC), and social marginal cost (SMC).

Model equations

  • Demand \( P = a - bQ \)
  • PMC \( P = c + dQ \)
  • MED \( \text{MED} = eQ \)
  • SMC \( P = c + (d + e)Q \)
