Constrained Utility Maximization
Identify and click on the point of constrained utility maximization.

Background

A consumer allocates income across two goods, \(x\) and \(y\), each at a given price. The three curves represent different levels of utility; the straight line represents the set of bundles the consumer can afford. The consumer's income is \( I = {{ I }} \), with prices \( P_x = {{ px }} \) and \( P_y = {{ py }} \).

Model equations

  • Utility \( U = x \cdot y \)
  • Budget \( P_x \, x + P_y \, y = I \)
  • IC \( y = \dfrac{U}{x} \)
