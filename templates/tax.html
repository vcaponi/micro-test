Microeconomics — Taxation
Unit Tax on Suppliers
A per-unit tax of t = {{ t }} is imposed on suppliers. Identify and click on the new market equilibrium.

Background

A per-unit tax collected from suppliers increases the cost of production for each unit sold. The original demand curve and the original supply curve are shown, along with the supply curve after the tax is applied.

Model equations

  • Demand \( P = \dfrac{a - Q}{b} \)
  • Supply \( P = \dfrac{Q + c}{d} \)
  • Tax \( t = {{ t }} \) per unit
  • Supply + tax \( P = \dfrac{Q + c}{d} + t \)
